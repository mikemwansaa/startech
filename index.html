About Us

Startech Communications is an authorized Motorola Solutions dealer and system integrator. We engineer robust, mission-critical voice and data networks for mining, security, government, and enterprise clients across Zambia.

From high-capacity MOTOTRBO radio systems deployed in subterranean mines to city-wide surveillance installations, we deliver turnkey solutions built for demanding African environments.

Our activity is shaped by constant technological change, which is why we remain committed to maintaining the highest standard of work — continuously training our engineers and technicians to position Startech Communications as one of the most trusted names in critical communications and security infrastructure in Zambia.

Core Services & Solutions

Enterprise-grade critical voice communications, RF tower engineering, electronic security systems, and remote power infrastructure — turnkey solutions built for demanding African environments.

Critical Voice & MOTOTRBO Systems

Digital two-way radio networks and repeater infrastructure, MOTOTRBO trunking (Capacity Plus / Capacity Max), dispatch consoles and GPS fleet tracking, plus intrinsically safe (ATEX/TIA) radios for hazardous mining and oil/gas environments.

CCTV & Integrated Security Systems

Enterprise IP video surveillance with thermal perimeter protection, automatic license plate recognition (ALPR/LPR), biometric access control and barrier automation, plus command-and-control room and video wall integration.

RF Engineering & Solar Tower Sites

Comprehensive site surveys, spectrum analysis and propagation modeling, heavy-duty lattice mast and guyed tower erection, high-throughput microwave backhaul links, and off-grid solar hybrid power for remote sites.

SLA Maintenance & 24/7 Support

Customized preventative maintenance contracts, drive testing, coverage optimization and antenna alignment, advanced battery management and diagnostics, and a local inventory of spares with rapid-response field technicians.
